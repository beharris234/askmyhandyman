You are running the weekly marketing session for this product.

1. Read `marketing/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md` and skim `marketing/` for the latest
   campaign plan, calendars, and reports so you know where things stand.
2. Ask the founder two questions (and only these): what shipped or changed
   in the product this week, and any numbers they have (signups, visits,
   replies). $ARGUMENTS may already contain answers — if so, don't re-ask.
3. Launch the **marketing-director** agent to update priorities for the week.
4. Launch the **social-media-manager** agent to produce this week's
   paste-ready content batch, folding in whatever shipped this week.
5. If the product changed in a way customers would care about, launch the
   **email-marketer** agent for an announcement email and the **copywriter**
   agent to update any affected site copy.
6. Close with a checklist of at most 5 human actions for the week, ordered
   by impact.
