## Who it's for (audiences)

1. **DJs** — promote gigs to their followers without giving out their number;
   track income per gig (DJ Tracker).
2. **Club owners / managers** — blast event announcements, guest-list drops,
   and last-minute promos to their VIP list (Owner + Club dashboards).
3. **Nightlife workers** (bartenders, dancers, promoters) — one private
   channel to their regulars; income tracker for tips/shifts.

## The problem it solves

Nightlife pros live off their following, but the options are bad: give out
your personal number (no privacy, no boundaries), or fight social media
algorithms that show your event post to 5% of followers. PrivateBlast gives
them a private one-way blast channel — their whole list sees every message,
and nobody gets their number.

## Key features to market

- One-tap blast to your entire list — 100% reach, no algorithm
- Total privacy — followers never see your phone number
- Built-in income tracker for gigs, tips, and shifts
- Installs like an app from a link — no app store, no download friction
- Club/owner dashboards for venue-level promotion

## Positioning & voice

- **Vibe:** nightlife-native, confident, a little exclusive. Dark, neon,
  after-midnight aesthetic (purple `#b496ff` → pink `#ff6eb4` gradient).
- **Voice:** short sentences. Direct. Insider tone — we know the scene.
  Never corporate, never "utilize", never exclamation-point spam.
- **Tagline:** "Your message. Nobody's business."

## Competitors / alternatives

- Instagram broadcast channels (algorithm-gated, not private, no income tools)
- Group texts (exposes your number, caps out, gets muted)
- Linktree + mailing list (clunky, not nightlife-native)

## Goals (edit per campaign)

- Primary: signups from nightlife pros (DJs first)
- Secondary: each pro brings their follower list (viral loop)

## Do NOT

- Promise end-to-end encryption unless we've verified we can claim it
- Use stock corporate imagery or generic SaaS language
- Target minors or promote alcohol consumption directly (market the pros' careers, not partying)
