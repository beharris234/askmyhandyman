Privacy Policy

BoneDropper · Last updated June 5, 2026

BoneDropper ("we," "us," the "app") helps you cook meat low and slow. This policy explains what we collect, how it's used, and your choices. We built BoneDropper to keep your data on your own device wherever possible.

What we collect

  • Cook data you create — the meats, weights, methods, ratings, notes, photos and videos you log. This is stored locally on your device (in your browser/app storage). We do not upload it to our servers, and we cannot see it.
  • Your cook profile — the "what kind of cook are you" answers and goals you pick. Stored locally on your device.
  • AI coach messages — when you use the "Big Drop" AI coach, the text and any photo you send are transmitted to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a reply. See "Third parties" below.
  • Basic technical data — like any website, our host may process your IP address and request metadata to deliver the app and protect against abuse.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not require an account, name, or email to use the app.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Third parties

  • Anthropic — the Big Drop coach is powered by the Claude API. Messages and photos you send to the coach are processed by Anthropic to produce a response, subject to Anthropic's policies. Per Anthropic's API terms, API inputs are not used to train their models by default.
  • Vercel — our hosting provider, which processes standard request data (e.g., IP, timestamps) to serve the app.

Photos & camera

Photos and videos you add to your cook log stay on your device. A photo is only sent off your device if you send it to the Big Drop coach for feedback.

Data retention & deletion

Because your cook log and profile live on your device, you control them. Deleting a cook removes it (and its photo) from your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes everything. Coach messages are not stored by us after a reply is returned.

Children

BoneDropper is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR/CCPA), you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Since we don't hold your cook data on our servers, most of it is already in your control on-device. For anything else, contact us below.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll change the date above when we do.
