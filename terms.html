What BoneDropper is

🔥 Food safety — read this

Cook times and temperatures in BoneDropper are estimates. The "pull temp" shown for tenderness is about texture, not safety. Always cook meat to a safe minimum internal temperature measured with a probe thermometer (e.g., poultry 165°F, ground meat 160°F, whole cuts of pork/beef/lamb 145°F then rest). Do not judge doneness or safety from time, appearance, or a photo alone. You are responsible for safe food handling, storage, and cooking.
